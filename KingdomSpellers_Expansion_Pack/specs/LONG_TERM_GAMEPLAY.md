# Kingdom Spellers long-term design

**Core loop**: create a word list (typed or photo), practice with a choice of modes, complete a mission, earn currency and mastery XP, build/decorate a living kingdom, unlock a new region, repeat with harder but age-appropriate challenges.

## Practice modes (release in stages)
- Learn: listen / syllables / meaning / copy example, no punishment.
- Spell: hear/show definition, type or drag letters, gentle retry hints.
- Word Explorer: synonyms/antonyms, definition matching, example sentences.
- Spelling Tower: a 5-stage mastery adventure with adaptive word review.
- Boss Challenge: review recently troublesome words; no combat or scary content.
- Daily Mini Quest: 3–5 minute optional practice, with forgiving streak system.

## Learning algorithm
Word status: new -> learning -> reviewing -> mastered. Review scheduling should depend on correctness and time since last practice; spelling errors should produce constructive feedback and revisit. Keep grade-dependent difficulty settings, but support parent overrides. Do not inflate spelling mastery based solely on easy practice completion.

## Progression philosophy
Starter: one humble cottage and a few build sites. Spelling earns coins; coins unlock classroom/academy, garden, marketplace and town hall. Cosmetic scenery is a fun coin sink that does not block educational progress. Building requirements include enough coins and educational milestones, not only grind. Castle and additional regions are higher milestones. Avatar ranks map to earned region milestones: Esquire, Knight, Baron/Baroness, Prince/Princess, King/Queen. Do not punish younger learners for slower achievement.

## Content runway
Season A (first 8 weeks): Greenwood plus 12 meaningful quests, 6 buildable structures, 8 decor pieces. Season B (months 3–4): Suncrest plus mini games. Season C (months 5–6): Stonehelm, more spelling modes. Season D (months 7–9): Tidemoor, more avatars and themed challenges. Season E (months 10–12): Emberreach and castle story arc. 12+ months: rotating optional chapters and player-designed kingdom decorations. This is a release design proposal, not existing implemented content.

## Sustainable design
Store quest templates independently of lists, allow generation of quests from a parent's words, sanitize user content, provide reliable saves and an easy restore flow. Avoid excessive grind, pay-to-win, forced social features or open chat for children.
