# Acceptance tests before approving a milestone

1. Fresh profile shows a genuine starter scene, not a complete castle town.
2. Each purchase shows eligibility, price, insufficient-funds feedback and current balance.
3. Double tapping Buy cannot deduct twice; no negative balance. Refresh maintains purchases.
4. A successful spelling activity saves progress and rewards exactly once.
5. Child can upload/photo-import a word list and review corrections before saving.
6. Owned/locked/in-progress art states are visually distinguishable; no missing textures.
7. Mobile width 375px and tablet 768px can scroll or pan/zoom without obscured controls.
8. Keyboard focus, labels, contrast and reduce-motion work; no sound required to play.
9. Images lazy load or are optimized; initial scene usable on a moderate mobile connection.
10. Old profiles load, prior saved structures do not disappear; migration notes reviewed.
11. Build/tests pass; console free of fatal errors; no secrets in repo.
12. Site assets and deep links work at www.kingdomspellers.com over HTTPS.
