# Kingdom Spellers — NPC, Rewards & Kingdom Decorations Pack

A practical, visual handoff for extending the approved illustrated fantasy art direction.

## Contents
- `assets/concept/01_game_expansion_art_board.png` — full game vision with named NPCs, kingdom decorations, reward screens and interfaces.
- `assets/reference_crops/` — zoomed reference sections for six NPC roles, scenery, interface, rewards and buildings.
- `assets/concept/13_prior_fantasy_ui_reference.png` and `14_prior_avatar_customization_reference.png` — matching visual reference from previous packs.
- `agent/AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` — implementation instructions to hand to VS Code agent.
- `agent/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` — suggested roles, ownership conditions, and intended screens.

**Important:** Individual crops are from a composite reference board; they are NOT transparent individual production sprites, and some contain surrounding captions. Do not place these crops directly as playable character art. Produce clean properly isolated sprites or programmatic scenes before integration. The full board is illustrative design direction, not a functioning screenshot.

## Design standards
Warm storybook rendering, cream parchment, deep royal blue/gold heraldry, lively warm-eyed characters, stone and timber architecture, bright greenery, layered paths and meaningful props. No blocky placeholder buildings, generic emoji NPCs, or wildly mismatched characters.

## Scope priority
1. Character roles with consistent storytelling and quest hooks.
2. Reward animations and readable, non-manipulative feedback.
3. World decoration layers connected to actual building ownership.
4. Cohesive maps, achievement cards, menus and progression.

Do not place secrets or test account credentials in code. Preserve account data, lists, mastery and inventory. Do not deploy without approval.
