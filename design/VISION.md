# Approved visual and product direction

## Core promise
Children restore a living fantasy kingdom by **learning to spell**. Spell first, earn the chance to build second. A strong game feel must not undermine literacy practice.

## Visual North Star
Premium 2D illustrated storybook fantasy: charming hand-painted feel, readable shapes and material detail. Village looks like a coherent environment, NOT flat colored squares, repetitive icon buildings, clip-art collages, minecraft blocks or disconnected UI cards.

Palette: pine green #2c654d, sunlit grass #8db565, warm parchment #fff3d6, dusk blue #506d86, roof terracotta #b95f45, warm stone #d8bd8f, ink #26332d, gold #e7b952. Light with clear contrast. No overuse of gold gradients or neon fantasy glows.

Composition: three-quarter camera, 2D or 2.5D layered scenery; main winding path connects old gate, starter cottage, expandable lots and riverbank; distant castle silhouette only if NOT already purchased (background silhouette must not imply ownership). Foreground foliage frames playable space, tree shadows anchor buildings, gentle atmospheric depth.

## Starting state matters
Before spending any resources, the kingdom is **not built**. Show a welcoming clearing with a shabby cottage/old well (if part of current starter state) and unmistakable build sites. Avoid showing palaces, fully formed towers, elaborate walls or unlocked regions on day one.

## Construction stages
1. Vacant site: subtle sign, timbers or outline, identifiable accessible label.
2. Affordable: inviting ribbon/glow without obscuring geography, price shown in adjacent detail panel.
3. Purchased: integrated illustrated structure with chimney, windows, shingles, stairs, flowerbeds, banners etc.
4. Upgraded: visually richer but same footprint and location; do not jump around.

## UI
A tidy HUD with current avatar, level/rank, practice entry, earnable resources, and clear next objective. Touch-friendly 44px+ targets, readable labels; children never need to parse tiny text. World remains center stage. Motion has reduced motion fallback. Daylight default; no faux-3D jagged polygons.

## Visual reference assets
